# Literature rights policy

Rights status is an editorial decision. The age of a work does not make it public domain inside this system.

## Statuses

- `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` — the editor has recorded that the work may be quoted here, with a note and a source.
- `LICENSED` — use is allowed under a recorded license, with attribution.
- `RESTRICTED` — metadata may be shown. No excerpt.
- `UNKNOWN` — rights are not settled. No excerpt.

## Excerpts

An excerpt is allowed only for `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` or `LICENSED`, must cite a published licensable source, and stays within 800 code points. Changing rights to `RESTRICTED` or `UNKNOWN` is rejected while excerpts exist. The public work page does not offer a control that pretends the full book can be read.

## What is not imported

No scraped books, no random corpora, and no long literary texts in production migrations. Development fixtures, when used, are local, short, and do not invent a real citation for a protected work. Full text without a recorded right is prohibited.
