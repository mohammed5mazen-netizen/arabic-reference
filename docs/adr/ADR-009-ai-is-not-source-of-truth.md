# ADR-009 AI Is Not Source of Truth

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-10-02

## Context

An assistant can draft meanings, diacritics, or explanations. Those drafts are not citations.

## Decision

Reviewed sources and editorial state are the authority. AI may assist readers and editors later. It must not publish linguistic facts, and it must not be stored as a source work.

## Consequences

S8 has to ground answers in the source model. A generated sentence without a citation remains unpublished.
